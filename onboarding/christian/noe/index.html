Noe's guide to Melody HQ

Melody HQ is the back office for Melody's app: online orders, rewards guests use, receipt claims, the to-do list and more. This page shows you every step. Keep it bookmarked.

1 · Log in

  1. Tap Open Melody HQ above (or go to sousshift.com/backoffice/christian/).
  2. Under the email box, tap “← Christian, Antidote or a manager? Pick your name”. (Don't use the email login or “New staff? Request access”: your login is already made.)
  3. In Who are you?, pick Noe (Melody staff).
  4. Type the password Antidote gave you. Capital letters and dashes count.
  5. Tap Log in. The top says Melody HQ and you only see Melody.

You stay signed in for a few hours. If it says “Signed out. Log in again.”, just log in again. Wrong password 10 times locks logins for 15 minutes.

2 · Put HQ on your home screen

iPhone (Safari): open HQ › tap the Share button › Add to Home Screen › Add.

Android (Chrome): open HQ › tap ⋮ › Add to Home screen › Add.

Now HQ opens like an app. Keep it open during your shift so new-order alerts can beep.

3 · The to-do list (top of every page)

  1. Red items need attention today; the others can wait.
  2. Tap an item. It jumps to the right tab.
  3. Fix it, then tap Done. You can add your own to-dos too (for example “order more QR table cards”).

4 · Online orders: receive and finish them

The 🛒 Orders tab shows today's paid pickup orders from the app. The tab number shows how many are waiting.

  1. A new order comes in: HQ beeps (and vibrates on Android) and a “New order” banner pops up. Tap it.
  2. Read the order: the guest's name, every item and note, and the pickup time. It's already paid by card, so don't charge it again.
  3. Ring it into Toast so the kitchen makes it (until Toast is connected, app orders don't print in the kitchen on their own).
  4. When the food is bagged, tap Ready. That marks it ready in HQ for everyone on shift.
  5. When the guest picks it up, tap Picked up. The order leaves the waiting list.

HQ checks for new orders every 30 seconds while it's open, so keep it open on a phone or tablet at the counter. Online ordering turns on when Christian says yes. Until then this tab stays empty.

5 · Redeem rewards at the table or register

Guests use rewards with a live pass on their phone. Nothing to type in HQ: you check the pass and ring the Toast button.

  1. The guest taps Use reward and then Show my pass now, with you there.
  2. Check the pass:
    • Today's date with the seconds ticking.
    • The countdown is running (starts at 10:00) and the colors move.
    • A one-time code like MLD-7K3P.
    • A white box with the exact Toast button name, like App Reward – Free Starter.
  3. Gray, “USED / EXPIRED”, or not moving? Don't accept it. A screenshot never moves. Kindly say it ran out and they can use a reward next visit.
  4. In Toast: for a free item, select that item on the check first ($-off rewards apply to the whole check). Tap Discounts › tap the button with the exact name on the pass.
  5. Type the pass code as the reason (or write it on the merchant copy). Done.

Every reward you redeem shows up in HQ › 🎟️ Rewards & claims with the code, the guest and the Toast button. Can't find the Discounts button in Toast? Ask Christian for the Toast permission.

6 · Receipt claims (“Add my visit”)

After paying, a guest can add points from their receipt: they type the check number and the total before tip in the app.

  1. Open 🎟️ Rewards & claims › Receipt claims. The to-do list also tells you when claims are waiting.
  2. Open Toast's sales report for that day. Find the same check number and make sure the total matches.
  3. Matches: nothing to do. Doesn't match, or the check doesn't exist: text Christian the claim (date, check # and guest) so he can flag it before anyone keeps those points.

Limits the app already enforces: one receipt per day, today's or yesterday's check only, each check once, up to $150.

7 · Help a guest join

  1. Point them to the QR code on the table or the window poster. The app opens in their browser; nothing to download.
  2. They type their first name and phone or email, tick the box and tap Get my reward.
  3. Don't type it for them and don't ask for their info: it's their phone.

Pitch line: “Scan to play the game and earn a free starter.”

8 · Prices, specials and discounts

You can open 💲 Prices & specials and 🏷️ Discounts. Every save is confirmed with a 6-digit code sent to your email, so send Antidote your email first. Until then you can look but not save.

  1. Change the value, tap Save, type the 6-digit code from your email.
  2. HQ then shows the matching step in Toast (for example, change the same price in Toast and Publish). The app only shows prices; guests pay what Toast charges.
  3. Christian gets an email about every change, and it's in the 🧾 Audit log.

Check with Christian before changing any price or discount. Never make free alcohol a reward (California law). Drink discounts are fine.

9 · Your daily routine

  1. Start of shift: log in, check the to-do list, keep HQ open for order alerts.
  2. During the shift: mark orders Ready and Picked up; check live passes before ringing rewards.
  3. End of day (2 min): compare receipt claims and rewards used with Toast's daily report. Send Christian any that don't match.

10 · Rules

  • Your login is only for you. Never share the password or let someone else use HQ as you.
  • Guest names, phones and emails stay in HQ: no screenshots, no texting them out.
  • One reward per visit, no stacking, rewards expire after 3 days.
  • Christian decides what your login can open. If a tab you need is missing, ask him (🔐 Team access).

Help

Questions about HQ or the app: text Antidote. Toast questions (buttons, permissions): ask Christian. HQ also has a ❓ Help tab with a Listen button.
