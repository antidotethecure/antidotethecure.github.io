4. Storm Run is a game of skill

Storm Run is a 45-second driving game. Your score depends on how well you play: you steer the food truck between lanes to grab coins and StormBurger food and to dodge lightning strikes, flying cows and stop signs, and you score for every second you stay on the road. Three hits end the run. Hazards and pickups appear in a varied pattern, so every run is different, but no prize in the game is awarded by chance: prizes depend only on your score. Money or items shown in the game are for scoring only.

  • Score prizes (sample, set by the Sponsor), one run over: 400 = Free house sauce; 600 = Free fries or onion rings; 800 = Free milkshake; 1,000 = Free Classic StormBurger. One prize at a time per member; a prize must be used within 3 days.
  • Weekly top score: the highest score from Monday through Sunday close wins a Free Double Classic StormBurger Combo. Ties go to the score posted first. The board resets every Monday.
  • Scores made with cheats, scripts, bots or multiple accounts are disqualified.
