# THE JAIL EPISODES — told by Antidote, 2026-09-17 (his account, his voice)

Part of the ANTIDOTE series (see life-movie-direction.md → SERIES FORMAT).
These scenes are Antidote's own testimony. On screen: no real names or
identifying details of other incarcerated individuals — composite/anonymous
figures ("a tall athletic dude," "an OG," "his little homie") exactly as he
tells it. His friend references stay as he gives them (FP / FT Hop Out,
Compton) pending their okay to be named.

## EPISODE: County — "The Joke"

- The dorm had a tall, athletic Black dude — mini afro, basketball-build,
  woke up every day doing push-ups, always working out. Everybody was cool
  with him. Didn't press the line or act gangster, but said he was a Blood
  from San Bernardino / IE area. He and Antidote became cool.
- One night they're shooting jokes at each other. Antidote starts getting
  the better of the joking — and a Hispanic guy LAUGHS at one of the jokes.
- **County rule:** you can't be joking on your own race and have another
  race jump in laughing — that can entice a riot. So the dude starts
  getting mad. Night ends "whatever."
- Next day: Antidote is talking privately with someone from his own city /
  section. The dude comes over eavesdropping. Antidote: "let us talk — we
  need to talk alone." The dude turns hostile: "I don't have to go nowhere...
  I'll knock your teeth out your mouth." (Still heated from the jokes.)
- The higher-ups saw it transpiring and set it: **they fight after the
  guard does his pass.** Antidote would rather have fought right then —
  the guy is big, athletic, in shape; you don't want to LET him prepare.
  But he waits the 15 minutes.
- **The prep (key cinematic detail):** wraps his hands with socks, puts
  tissue in his mouth, walks into the little alleyway between the bunk
  beds. Guard passes out of sight. "Go."
- **First 5–10 seconds:** Antidote catches him with a right hand to the
  jaw — the dude goes straight to sleep, never lands a punch, leans
  asleep into a bunk. Antidote hits him a couple more; the Bloods jump in
  and pull him off — "nah Blood, we can't let you do the homie like that."
- They wake the dude up and make them **fight again.** Second round: the
  dude clips him a couple times but never knocks him down. They get it
  out of the way. Everybody gives Antidote his respect for standing his
  ground.

## EPISODE: The Bus — "They Think I Lost"

- The very next day: prison transfer. On the bus with random new people,
  face beat up, red, black eye. They can tell he fought — and they assume
  he LOST. Assume he's a punk. The win in county makes him look like a
  TARGET everywhere the bus goes. (Cruel irony — carry this in narration.)

## EPISODE: Reception — "The Setup"

- On the way in, a guy acts like he's cool with him. At reception, that
  guy sets him up: tells people in the dorm Antidote was talking about
  them, tells his business, politicks on him.
- Antidote ends up called to fight an OG — who says he's too old to
  fight, "so you fight my little homie." Mid-fight, the little homie's
  homeboy jumps in — **two on one.** A punch splits his eyebrow open —
  a big gouge. He looks in the mirror leaking, didn't even know he was
  bleeding like that. They stop it because blood is going everywhere.
  He's still ready to fight, ready to handle his business.
- Back at his bunk, patched up. **What he was actually doing in there:
  making movies. Writing movie scripts. Scary movies.** Talking to an
  older OG who knew about movie plugs. He was trying to mind his own
  business and create — the fighting kept finding him "because of my
  energy and who I am." (SERIES NOTE: he was writing movies in prison,
  and now his life IS the movie. That's the thesis of these episodes.)

## EPISODE: The Riot

- That dorm ends up in a riot. Police pepper spray everybody. Blacks and
  Mexicans separated. All the Blacks sitting in a cell, handcuffed behind
  their backs, pepper sprayed, sniffing, eyes burning — waiting **45
  minutes to an hour** just to get water in their eyes.

## EPISODE: The Yard — "Laps in the Rain"

- One rainy morning he skips yard roll call (the morning "come see
  everybody" for your city) — him and his bunkie both. Discipline: run
  five laps (roughly a mile and change; ~3 laps to a mile). He's not in
  shape at the time. Running, talking to his boy **FP / FT Hop Out from
  Compton**, slowing to a jog, being told to keep running.
- Passing a group from his city (Long Beach), he says what's up to the
  main guy — and another dude gets mad Antidote didn't greet HIM. Calls
  him out to fade over a hello.
- **The pinky:** the day before, playing basketball, he tried to stop an
  old man's full-court throw, hand up wrong — fractured pinky, splint on.
- He fights the dude in the dorm anyway — holds his ground, basically
  gets the better of him. Then, because he doesn't gangbang and made a
  banger look bad: **"you gotta get DP'd" — fight four or five dudes.**
- He refuses: "I just fought somebody, I'm injured — y'all on some
  bullying." Goes to the neutral bunk area. They make it big: dudes at
  the window — "cuz, you're making this look real bad, you gotta go
  handle your business." His answer (keep in the film): "I just fought
  for basically nothing, and now y'all want me to fight four or five
  guys for no reason. That don't make sense."
- They jump him. **A blow breaks/fractures his eye socket** — left eye
  swells into a ball like a baseball; every time he blows his nose it
  puffs up with liquid and air and he has to push it back down. Nose
  bloody, can't even blow it.
- Kicked off the yard for it. While the officer has him rolling it up,
  dudes on the other side of the dorm are grabbing his stuff — food,
  snacks — robbing what they can. He runs over and saves some of it.
- **The cage:** they put him in a cage downstairs on the yard where
  everybody can see him — bloodied, eye ruined, holding what's left of
  his property — and everybody walks by looking. "Damn, that's crazy.
  That's him." (KEY IMAGE of the whole saga: the cage, in view of the
  yard, holding his own life in his hands. Storyboard this one.)

## EPISODE: SNY — "The Rap Battle"

- Fractured pinky = medical hold, can't leave the prison. Level 2 can't
  go down to level 1 — so they send him UP to a level 3 **SNY yard**
  (the "PC yard" — where people go labeled snitches or who can't be in
  general population). He had to deal with everything extra there and
  protect himself.
- Luckily, no real fights there. Instead: **a rap battle** — the yard's
  known rapper got mad Antidote didn't give him a piece of cheese, so it
  became a whole rap battle in front of the dorm. **Antidote beat him —
  "and that was not easy."** He defended himself with the exact talent
  the whole series is about. (SERIES NOTE: the saga that began with a
  joke ends with a rap battle — the mouth that got him into it gets him
  out of it. That's the arc of these episodes.)

## EPISODE: The Appeal — "It Was Weed"

- He appealed his case — **he was in there for weed** — and won out.
  Got out of jail, then went into his **social equity** work (the weed
  charge and the social-equity chapter are cause and effect: the same
  plant that caged him became the legal business lane he pursued).

## WHY THESE EPISODES MATTER (series framing)

Every fight in here is the same story: a man who didn't gangbang, who was
writing movie scripts on his bunk, who kept getting pulled into other
people's rules — and stood his ground every single time, alone, with a
fractured pinky, with a broken eye socket, in a cage in front of the
yard. The discipline threads (books, Neville, vocabulary) run right
through these cells. The audience must leave these episodes knowing: the
peace he has now was PAID for.
