3. Melody Approach

Melody Approach is an arcade flying game with two modes. Campaign: 10 levels of about 45 seconds over LA, each a little harder, with six boss fights (levels 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10), then a landing at LAX. Survival: no finish line; the game keeps getting harder until your plane goes down, and your time survived is what counts. You shoot down jets, UFOs, choppers and the bosses, dodge their fire (every attack is warned first), and catch Melody's food and music notes. Guns go live about 20 seconds into a flight; beating levels and bosses (or, in Survival, flying longer) unlocks stronger guns, which use limited ammo refilled by catching ammo crates. Three hits and your plane goes down (the game is over, nothing else happens). Players can tap the ⚔️ button to switch to a calm flight with no combat.

  • Free to play, with or without an account. No purchase necessary.
  • BETA: game rewards are switched OFF until Sponsor turns them on in its back office. While they are off, the game gives no prizes, codes, discounts or rewards points; the weekly boards rank players for bragging rights only.
  • Two weekly boards, both reset every Monday: Campaign (ranked by score) and Survival (ranked by the longest time survived). Practice and test flights don't go on the boards.
  • Once Sponsor turns rewards on: your score turns into rewards points (1 point per 100 score, at most 50 game points a day, Los Angeles date), and the top player on each weekly board may win the weekly prize shown in the game (for example, free Melody Fries). One code per visit, one prize per day.
  • One code per visit: every pass gets a fresh one-time code for that visit. It works once and expires 10 minutes after the pass opens. A used or expired code never works again; your next code comes on your next visit.

Winning a gift and using that same gift the same day counts as one prize. A second prize that day shows "Come back tomorrow"; your next prize is available tomorrow. 7. Nearby alerts and the one-time "Location buddy" bonus

  • Optional. In the app, "Turn on nearby alerts" asks your phone for location and notification permission. Nothing is turned on without your tap and your phone's permission.
  • What we collect: your phone's precise location (California treats it as sensitive personal information) and whether location and notifications are on. When: only while the app is open on your screen. A web app can't track you in the background, and we don't. Why: to show a "You're nearby" deal when you open the app within about half a mile of Melody Bar & Grill, and to count days for the bonus. Your exact location stays on your phone; the rewards system only records, by date, that location and notifications were on. Never sold or shared, and not used for ads or any other purpose.
  • Bonus: +100 points, once per member, when location and notifications were both on when you opened the app on at least 3 different days spanning at least 7 days. It's added automatically. It can't be earned again, including by turning location off and back on, using another phone, or rejoining.
  • Notifications: on iPhone they need the app on your Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer). Any reminders we send are scheduled (for example lunch or happy hour); we can't send "you just walked past" alerts.
