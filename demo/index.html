On the call

  1. Open this page, tap the right demo.
  2. Not ready for a call? Text them /demo/start/. They fill it out, you get an iMessage, approve it, and their sample app is live.
  3. Want them to sell themselves? Text them /demo/growth/?for=Their%20Name. It shows rewards, CRM, outreach and a growth calculator.
  4. Scroll the menu — the food photos sell it before you say a word.
  5. Tap the game, play 10 seconds, show the prize board.
  6. Say the line: "I built this for you — want the real version with your phone number and your logo?"
  7. They say yes? Tap Draft the agreement (bottom of their demo, or the blue button here). Founding deal is pre-filled: type their name + email, they sign on your phone, you sign, Save PDF.
