Antidote Enterprises LLC, doing business as SousShift AI ("we"), builds menu, rewards and game apps for restaurants and small businesses. This policy covers the information our business clients give us and the text line we run for them.

What we collect

  • Business clients: name, business name, email, mobile number, and the menu, photos and settings you send us to build your app.

How we use it

To build and run your app, provide support, send account messages you opted in to, and bill for our services.

Text messages (SMS)

We share information only with service providers that help us run the service (hosting, messaging and payment providers), when the law requires it, or with your permission. We never sell personal information.
