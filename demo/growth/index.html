What you get

Three systems that work together

Most places have none of these. Big chains pay for all three separately. You get them built as one, around your menu and your brand.

01 · E-Rewards

A rewards program people actually want to use

Paper punch cards get lost in wallets. A game with their name on the leaderboard gets opened every visit.

  • Custom game made around your food. Your dishes, your colors, your logo. Customers play while they wait for their order.
  • Stars for every visit and order. Stars turn into the rewards you choose: a free side, money off, a free meal.
  • Weekly prize board. Top score of the week wins. Resets every week, so people come back to defend their spot.
  • You set the rewards and the cost. Change prizes anytime: push a high-margin item, clear slow inventory, reward big spenders.
  • Fun at the table, faster turns. Waiting feels shorter, kids are busy, and nobody's flagging down a server to ask about the menu.
02 · Customer CRM

Finally know who your customers are

Right now most of your guests walk out and you never see their name. Every sign-up becomes a customer profile you own.

  • One customer list, built automatically. Name, phone or email, first visit, last visit, stars, and birthday month, with no typing by your staff.
  • See your regulars and your VIPs. Who comes in most, who spends most, who just tried you for the first time.
  • Spot who's slipping away. Flags customers who haven't been back in 30+ days, before they're gone for good.
  • Know what's working. Sign-ups, return visits and rewards cashed in, in a simple check-in with your numbers.
  • Your data, not ours. If you ever leave, we hand your full customer list over in a standard file. It's your business asset.

03 · Customer outreach

Messages that fill seats on slow days

Instagram shows your post to a fraction of your followers. A message to your own list reaches the people who already like your food.

  • Automatic win-back. Customers who haven't been in for a while get a "we miss you" offer on their own, with nothing for you to send.
  • Birthday rewards. A free dessert or side in their birthday month. People rarely celebrate alone, so they bring the table.
  • One-tap slow-day blasts. Dead Tuesday? Send "double stars tonight" to everyone on your list and watch it pick up.
  • New item and special launches. Tell your regulars first. They're the ones most likely to come try it.
  • More Google reviews. A one-tap "review us on Google" button right in the game, with your latest reviews on show. Asked while the meal is still fresh.
  • "You're nearby" alerts. Customers who turn on location see your spot on a map and get a "stop by, you're close" offer when they're within a couple of miles.
  • Opt-in and respectful. Customers choose to join and can stop anytime. No spam, no bought lists.

Questions owners ask

Quick answers

Do my customers have to download an app?

No. They scan the QR on the table and it opens in their phone's browser. Nothing to install.

Does my staff have to do anything?

No. No punching cards, no typing in phone numbers, no extra steps at the register. Stars, sign-ups and messages all run on their own.

Who owns the customer list?

You do. If you ever cancel, we hand over your customer and player data in a standard file format.

Will it spam my customers?

No. Customers opt in, messages are spaced out and about things they care about (their stars, their birthday, your specials), and they can stop anytime.

How fast can it go live?

Your demo is usually built in an evening. Once you sign and send your menu, it can be on your tables within the week.
