Play & earn pointsFly the Melody approach

A night dogfight over LA. Campaign: 10 levels that follow a real SoCal route in six parts: take off past Melody and over Inglewood, ride the 105 freeway to LAX, Nalu Vida on the Venice Pier, Compton, Long Beach and the Queen Mary, then Hollywood for the finale, each a little harder, with six bosses (an Army gunship, the Super Jet, a big jet and its escorts, a rocket ship, an army base and a final flock of white birds), then land back at LAX. Survival: no finish line, it just keeps getting harder: how long can you last? Catch Melody's food as it floats by. Drag anywhere on the game and the plane glides to your finger; tap ⚡ for a speed boost and grab the hourglass for slow-mo. Prefer a calm flight? Pause and turn combat off.
