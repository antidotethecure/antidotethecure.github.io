From Tulsa to Lomita

Pitmaster Jon Harry Bayouth was born and raised in Tulsa, Oklahoma and learned from Oklahoma's smokehouse masters. His mantra is slow and low: homemade dry rub, hickory smoked 6 to 14 hours on a Southern Pride smoker, finished with Harry's Original sauce. He runs Harry's with his wife Margot Dean.
