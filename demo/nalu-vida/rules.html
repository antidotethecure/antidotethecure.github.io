1. Sponsor

Nalu Vida, 1 Washington Blvd, Venice, CA 90292 ("Sponsor"). [Sponsor to confirm legal business name.] App and game provided by SousShift AI.

2. One simple points program

  • Joining Nalu Vida Rewards is free. One account per person. Nalu Vida has its own points balance; points don't move between restaurants.
  • 10 points per $1 on eligible purchases (before tip). Until Toast is connected: tap "Add my visit" in the app and snap your receipt or type the check number, total and date (today or yesterday). One receipt per member per day, each check once, up to $150 per check. Claims are checked against the restaurant's sales report and points from made-up checks are removed. Once Toast is connected this is automatic and your points carry over. [Sponsor to list what is not eligible, e.g. tax, tip, gift cards, alcohol if required.]
  • Bonuses, each capped: +100 once when you join · +250 once a year on your birthday · +100 per friend who joins with your code and makes a first visit · up to +50 a day from the game (see 3).
  • There is no daily spin, no prize wheel, no lucky draw, no tokens and no second currency.

3. Ride the Nalu

Ride the Nalu is a surfing game on the Venice Pier loop: 5 levels of paddling out and riding the wave in. You steer around sea life and grab food rafts; bumping into anything is a mild wipeout (a splash, then you pop back up). There are no weapons and no boss fights.

  • Free to play, with or without an account. No purchase necessary.
  • Your score turns into rewards points: 1 point per 100 score, at most 50 game points a day (Los Angeles date). Not a member yet? Your points from today are held on your phone; join the same day to save them.
  • The game gives no prizes, codes or discounts. The weekly board is for bragging rights only and resets every Monday.
  • Scores made with cheats, scripts, bots or multiple accounts don't count.

4. Rewards

Using a reward: tap "Use reward" and show the live pass to your server, who applies it in Toast. The points come off when you open the pass. A pass works once and expires 10 minutes after it opens; one reward per member per day.

One code per visit, one prize per day.
  • One code per visit: every pass gets a fresh one-time code for that visit. It works once and expires 10 minutes after the pass opens. A used or expired code never works again; your next code comes on your next visit.
  • One prize per day (Los Angeles date), per member and per phone: a reward, an early-bird / founding-member gift, or anything won in a game, spin or draw. Winning a gift and using that same gift the same day counts as one prize. A second prize that day shows "Come back tomorrow"; your next prize is available tomorrow. Games stay free to play for fun and score.
  • Referral codes: one friend's code per member, entered once when you join; it can't be changed or swapped later, and you can't use your own. Referral points pay once per friend who joins with your code and makes a first visit. A referral never stacks with another reward on the same visit.
  • These limits are checked by our server, not just your phone, so a second phone, a cleared browser or a screenshot doesn't get around them.

  • What's recorded each time you open the app (at most once every 10 minutes): the time, a short device summary (for example "iPhone · iOS 18 · Safari"), whether you opened it from your Home Screen, a random ID for this phone, and your member ID if you joined.
  • Approximate location only if you've already turned on location for this app. The app never asks for location just for this. If location is on, it records one approximate spot (rounded to about 1 km) at the moment you open the app. Nothing runs in the background. Turn location off for this site/app in your phone's Settings to stop it.
