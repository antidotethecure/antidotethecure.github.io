How the AI phone agent works

☎️ It picks up every call

  1. Your restaurant gets its own local number, or we forward your current line to it when you're busy or closed.
  2. A natural-sounding AI voice answers in your restaurant's name, 24/7, in English or Spanish.
  3. It answers the questions you get all day: hours, address, parking, menu, prices, specials, gluten-free, kids menu, catering, karaoke night.
  4. It texts the caller your app link so they can see the menu, order, and join rewards.
  5. Reservations and big orders: it takes the name, party size and time, then texts and emails you right away.
  6. "Can I talk to a person?" It transfers the call to you or takes a message.
  7. Every call is logged with a short summary, so you see what people ask most.

✉️ AI email that brings people back

  1. Answers your inbox: catering asks, event questions, "are you open on the holiday", drafted in your voice. You approve, or let it send.
  2. Win-back emails: anyone who hasn't come in for 30 days gets a "we miss you" note with a reward to come back.
  3. Birthday emails the morning of, with the treat code.
  4. Monthly level-ups: "2 more visits this month for VIP."
  5. Specials blasts: "Karaoke Wednesday", "Taco Tuesday", new dish drops.
  6. Everyone who signs up in your app (phone or email) is on the list automatically.
  7. Unsubscribe is built in and we follow the email and texting rules for you.
