# ANTIDOTE — Music Video Treatment

*A spiritual sci-fi short film built around one man, his breaking wings, and the reveal that everyone he tried to save was himself.*

---

## LOGLINE

A man strains to lift every soul he's ever loved into the light. Each time he reaches down, his wings tear violently out of his own skin under the weight. He finally releases the people dragging him back — only to discover, in the last frame, that every face he was reaching for was his own.

---

## THE ONE-SENTENCE ENGINE

**Every act of love costs him altitude.** That is the entire video. He rises by lifting others; he bleeds every time he does; and at the end he learns the weight was never theirs — it was his, because they were never separate from him.

---

## VISUAL LANGUAGE & TONE

- **Genre feel:** cinematic, photoreal, spiritual sci-fi. Think a Compton street at 3am that opens into deep space.
- **Palette:**
  - *The world / the fallen:* cold desaturated blues, ash-grey, sodium-orange streetlight, murk. "Muddy."
  - *The awakening / the wings:* warm gold, bone-white light, prismatic third-eye violet.
  - *The reveal:* mirror-silver, infinite reflections, black void studded with light-nodes (Indra's Net).
- **Texture:** grain, real skin, sweat, dust in light shafts. Nothing glossy or fake — the divinity has to live in a *real* body.
- **Camera:** slow, deliberate, weighty. Long push-ins on the face. Only the wing-tears and the "pull-down" moments are violent and fast. The calm makes the violence land.
- **Wing rule (important):** wings are always earned and always cost something. They are muscle and bone and feather ripping *out of his back through the skin* — not a costume. Every time he reaches DOWN to save someone, the strain tears them further.

---

## THE WING MECHANIC (the spine of the edit)

Track the wings as their own character across the whole video:

1. **Forming** (Verse 1) — wet, new, pushing through the skin of his back for the first time. Tender, raw.
2. **First tear** (Hook) — he reaches down for someone, they grab his arm, he strains upward, a feather rips loose and a gash opens.
3. **Violent rupture** (Verse 2, the "pull-down") — multiple hands drag him toward the pit; the wings peel open, bleeding, bone showing.
4. **The release** — he lets the hands go. The wings, half-destroyed, catch air anyway.
5. **Reformed / scarred** (after "found God in myself") — they grow back stronger but marked, no longer pure white.
6. **Still** (Tag) — they stop moving. We don't know if he's ascended or died.
7. **Dissolve** (final reveal) — the wings and the body dissolve into light-nodes; every node is a face; every face is his.

---

## CASTING / WHO APPEARS

- **HIM** — the artist. One consistent character across every scene. *(Generate one character reference first and reuse it everywhere — see Production Notes.)*
- **THE FALLEN** — mom, dad, buddies, ex-girlfriends, cousins. Do NOT make them monsters. They're ordinary, loved, and *clinging*. Their demons are shown as shadow-tethers, not horns.
- **THE REVEAL SELVES** — in the finale, each of the fallen turns and has HIS face. Same character reference, different wardrobe/age.

---

## WARDROBE ARC (the material → the divine)

Two looks, one transformation. The wardrobe IS the story:

- **[STREET]** *(scenes 00–07)* — the materialistic Antidote, lost and
  searching for love: fitted black cap, heavy gold chain, iced-out watch,
  graphic tee under an open designer jacket. Everything he's chasing is on
  his body. Use in prompts: *"wearing a designer urban streetwear fit —
  fitted black cap, heavy gold chain, iced-out watch, graphic tee under an
  open designer jacket"*
- **THE SHEDDING** *(scene 07)* — when he releases the cords, he loses the
  materialistic things WITH them: the gold chain snaps and falls into the
  pit, the cap and torn jacket fall away. He rises stripped, bare, holding
  nothing.
- **[ROBE]** *(scenes 08–13, from "found God in myself")* — the awakened
  self, no jewelry, nothing left to chase: *"wearing a flowing off-white
  linen messiah robe, a crown of thorns on his head, thin trickles of blood
  running down his face, no jewelry"*

Body base for every prompt: *"a big, broad-shouldered heavyset Black man
with light brown skin, freckles across his face, and a chinstrap beard"*

## RECURRING MOTIFS

- **The projector & the shadow** — reality is a movie he's projecting from his third eye. Open and close on this.
- **Shadow-tethers** — the fallen are chained to their past by black cords wrapped around their own ankles/wrists. They hold the cords themselves.
- **The unanswered call** — the Hook's "call me" shown as light reaching across a plane of consciousness, ringing out, no answer.
- **The scale** *(from Midnight Gospel)* — a soul weighed on a balance. Optional cutaway.
- **Indra's Net** — the final image. Infinite reflective nodes, each holding a version of him.

### 00 — COLD OPEN (intro / before first line)

**Description:** Total black. A single shaft of light. He sits cross-legged, eyes closed, meditating. As the beat drops, light beams out of his forehead (third eye) and *projects* the room around him into existence — the walls, the objects, all cast from his mind like a film onto a wall.

**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Cinematic wide shot, a big broad-shouldered heavyset Black man in a fitted black cap, heavy gold chain and designer streetwear, seen half in silhouette, sitting cross-legged in meditation in a pitch-black void, a single beam of warm light from above. A violet-white beam of light emits from his forehead and projects a faint room into existence around him like a film projector casting onto fog. Slow push-in. Photoreal, volumetric light, dust particles, film grain, spiritual sci-fi, 4k, shallow depth of field.

---

### 01 — VERSE 1: THE STUDY / THE ISOLATION

**Lyric anchor:** *"Studying the things I've studied / makes life muddy"* … *"I played wit lasers / and frozen water molecules"*

**Description:** His lab and his cell in one. Papers, redacted CIA-style documents pinned to the wall, a laser splitting through frozen water molecules, a candle, a meditation cushion worn from hours of use. He hasn't slept. This is the *work* that earned him the right to speak.

**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Cinematic shot of a big broad-shouldered heavyset Black man with freckles and a chinstrap beard, wearing a fitted black cap, heavy gold chain, iced-out watch and designer streetwear, alone in a dim cluttered study at night, redacted government documents pinned to the wall, a green laser beam refracting through a suspended frozen water molecule sculpture, candlelight, papers everywhere, exhausted intense eyes. Cold blue and amber light. Slow dolly across the room. Photoreal, moody, film grain, volumetric haze, 4k.

### 02 — VERSE 1: LOSING EVERY SOUL

**Lyric anchor:** *"Had to lose every single soul / mom, dad, buddies"*

**Description:** He stands with his hand extended. One by one, the people he loves — mother, father, friends — turn their backs and walk into grey fog. He reaches, they don't take his hand. First wisp of a wing pushing wet through the skin of his back.

**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Cinematic shot, a big heavyset Black man with freckles and a chinstrap beard, in designer streetwear with a heavy gold chain, standing with one hand reaching out, as several ordinary people (older woman, older man, young friends) slowly turn their backs and walk away into grey fog. His face is anguished. A single wet white feathered wing begins pushing raw through the skin of his upper back, blood at the base. Desaturated cold palette, backlight, slow motion, photoreal, emotional, film grain, 4k.

### 03 — VERSE 1: WAKE UP, REMEMBER YOU'RE THE CREATOR

**Lyric anchor:** *"You've been sleep for so long / now wake up / and remember you're the creator"*

**Description:** He touches a sleeping figure's forehead. Light tries to pour from his hand into them. They flinch awake, reject it, pull back into shadow. The rejection is physical — the light recoils.

**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Cinematic close shot, a big heavyset Black man with freckles and a chinstrap beard, in urban streetwear and a heavy gold chain, gently pressing two fingers to the forehead of a sleeping person, radiant golden light flowing from his hand. The sleeping person suddenly recoils, the light snaps back, shadow swallows them again. Chiaroscuro lighting, gold against deep blue-black, slow motion, photoreal, spiritual, film grain, shallow depth of field, 4k.

### 04 — HOOK: THE PLANE OF CONSCIOUSNESS / THE CALL

**Lyric anchor:** *"Kinda like it if you called me / cause I'm so over being lonely"* … *"if you believe me / I guess I'll get on a plane / fly to your city"*

**Description:** He stands on a thin plane of light suspended in deep space — the "plane" is literal, a plane of consciousness. He reaches across the void toward a distant figure. A line of light stretches between them like a call trying to connect. It rings. No answer.

**Higgsfield prompt:**

### 05 — HOOK: THE FIRST TEAR

**Lyric anchor:** *"hold me console me / and then I'll leave without a trace"*

**Description:** He descends and grips a person's arm to pull them up. They cling but won't rise — deadweight. He strains upward; the wing wrenches; a feather rips out and a gash opens across his back. Blood on white. He begins to go semi-transparent — "leave without a trace."

**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Cinematic dynamic shot, a winged heavyset Black man in designer streetwear, his heavy gold chain swinging, straining upward pulling a limp clinging person by the arm, his white wings wrenching under the weight, feathers ripping loose, a bloody gash tearing open across his back. His body flickering semi-transparent like he's dissolving. Harsh backlight, gold and crimson, motion blur, slow motion, visceral, photoreal, film grain, 4k.

### 06 — VERSE 2: THE PULL-DOWN (MUTUAL GRAVITY)

**Lyric anchor:** *"your soul would not let me come in / you had a guard up… you just want to be disloyal"*

**Description:** The core image. He's mid-air, wings spread, trying to rise. Below, a pit. Many hands and black shadow-cords reach up and wrap his ankles, dragging him down while he strains up. The people pulling him are chained to their *own* ankles by the same cords — they're holding themselves down and taking him with them.

**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Cinematic vertical shot, a winged heavyset Black man in torn designer streetwear suspended mid-air straining to fly upward while dozens of desperate hands and black shadowy cords reach up from a dark pit below, clutching at his heavy gold chain and tearing his designer jacket, wrapping around his ankles and dragging him down. The people below are bound by the same black cords around their own limbs. His wings peeling open violently, bone visible, bleeding. Dramatic downlight, blue-black pit, gold above. Epic, visceral, slow motion, photoreal, film grain, 4k.

### 07 — VERSE 2: THE RELEASE

**Lyric anchor:** *"because if they wouldn't repent / I couldn't let them slide"* (callback) / instrumental beat

**Description:** He makes the choice. He opens his hands and lets the cords slip. The people fall back into the pit — not thrown, *released*. And the materialistic things go WITH them: the gold chain snaps and falls into the pit, the cap and torn jacket fall away. He loses the chasing itself. His half-destroyed wings catch air. He rises stripped bare, holding nothing. Grief, not triumph.

**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Cinematic shot, a winged heavyset Black man opening his hands and releasing black shadowy cords, his heavy gold chain snapping off his neck and falling into the dark pit along with his cap and torn designer jacket, letting the clinging figures fall gently back below him. He is left bare-chested in torn cloth, holding nothing. His face full of grief not anger. His torn bleeding wings catching air and beginning to lift him. Slow motion, single tear, gold rim light against darkness, photoreal, emotional, film grain, 4k.

Deep space backdrop, god rays, lens flare, photoreal, epic, cinematic, 4k.

### 12 — FINAL HOOK: THE REVEAL (they were all him)

**Lyric anchor:** *"and then I'll leave without a trace"* (the true meaning: "I was always you")

**Description:** The Midnight Gospel move. He stands among all the fallen he tried to save. One by one, each of them turns toward the camera — and every single face is *his own face*. Dawning realization on his face. Surreal, dreamlike, cool silver light, slow motion, photoreal, uncanny, film grain, 4k.

**Higgsfield prompt (part B — Indra's Net dissolve):**
> Cinematic surreal shot, a heavyset Black man in an off-white messiah robe dissolving into particles of light that scatter into an infinite three-dimensional web of glowing mirrored nodes stretching endlessly in all directions, each node containing a tiny reflection of the same man, each reflecting all the others. Cosmic, infinite, silver and gold, sacred geometry, Indra's Net. Slow pull back into infinity. Photoreal, epic, ethereal, 4k.

## HIGGSFIELD PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Character consistency:** Before generating scenes, create ONE character reference of the artist (a "Soul" / trained character in Higgsfield, or a single strong portrait you reuse). Feed it into every prompt so it's the same man in every shot. This is the #1 thing that makes it look like a real film instead of 13 different people.
- **Wing consistency:** Decide the wing look once (white → scarred) and describe them the same way each time. Keep the "tearing out of the skin, blood at the base" language for the violent beats.
- **Generate in passes:** Do all the *calm* shots first (00, 01, 08, 11, 13) to lock the character and tone, then the *violent* wing shots (05, 06, 07), then the *cosmic* shots (04, 09, 10, 12).
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 if this is the main YouTube cut; also grab 9:16 versions of shots 05, 06, 09, 12 for Shorts/Reels teasers.
- **Motion:** Higgsfield's camera-motion presets — use slow push-ins and orbits for the sacred shots, and let the wing-tear shots run faster/handheld.

---

## EDIT / CAPCUT NOTES

- Cut the calm on the beat's downbeats; save every **wing-tear** for a lyrical hit ("lose every single soul," "leave without a trace," the pull-down).
- Color-grade the three worlds distinctly (muddy blue / gold awakening / silver reveal) so the audience feels the shift without being told.
- Hold the **Tag** ("no one noticed") in near silence and stillness — let it breathe before the final reveal detonates.
- The reveal (scene 12) should be the loudest visual moment even if the music is quiet there. Contrast sells it.
