💳 Your register (POS): no headache

Nothing to install · no downtime

The app works with whatever register you already use (Toast, Square, Clover, SpotOn, anything). We don't replace it, rewire it, or shut anything down.

1
Day one: zero setup. When a member pays, staff tap "Ring up a member" on any phone or tablet: scan the guest's code (or type their phone), enter the check total. Points, rewards and your sales numbers all log automatically.
2
Optional: discount buttons on your register (about 10 minutes, we do it with you). We add 2–3 buttons like "App reward" so rewards ring up cleanly. Step-by-step guides are ready for every major register.
3
Later, if you want it: a direct connection so app guests are matched to checks automatically. We handle it end to end; your staff keeps working the same way.
