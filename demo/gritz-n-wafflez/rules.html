3. No purchase necessary

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO PLAY ORDER UP!, TO SPIN THE DAILY WHEEL, OR TO WIN A PRIZE. A PURCHASE DOES NOT IMPROVE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING.

  • Daily Spin: always free. One spin per member per day (Los Angeles time). Spins are never sold, never cost tokens and cannot be earned by buying anything.
  • Order Up! (current version): free to play for members, as often as you like.
  • Order Up! with tokens (possible future version): if the Sponsor turns on game tokens, a play costs 10 tokens. Free ways to get tokens, equal to or better than buying food: 10 free tokens every week (one free play), added automatically the first time you open the app that week; and 10 free tokens on request once a day (one free play a day) using the "Free tokens" button, no purchase necessary. Tokens also come as a loyalty bonus with food purchases (1 token per $1). Tokens are never sold on their own, have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and cannot be exchanged for cash or prizes.

4. Order Up! is a game of skill

Your score depends only on how well you run the kitchen: points for every correct item and special request, a speed score measured to the tenth of a second from the moment a table sits down, a quality score for how close each fried, pressed or skillet item is to perfectly cooked, a streak multiplier for perfect plates in a row, a bonus for serving a whole table together, tips from guests (based on your speed, cooking and their mood), and a bonus for every level you clear. Points come off for undercooked, charred or cold food, wrong plates and tables that walk out. Each level lasts 90 seconds; three walk-outs end the run. Before playing you pick a mode. 🧑 Adult mode runs at regular speed. 🧒 Kid mode (meant for ages 12 and under; it is self-selected and no age is asked or stored) is deliberately slow at level 1 (guests wait about two minutes, a new party arrives about every 18 seconds and food cooks more slowly) and gets faster each level until level 5 (about 40 seconds of patience, a party about every 6 seconds); its speed score is measured against each level's pace. Each mode has its own prize scores, best score and weekly board. Guests, orders and tip personalities appear in a varied sequence so every shift is different, but no prize in the game is awarded by chance: prizes depend only on your score.

  • Score prizes (sample, set by the Sponsor), one run over:
    🧑 Adult mode: 3,500 = Free Kiki Palmer; 6,500 = Free side of Smackin' Mac; 10,000 = Free Fried Cheese Gritz; 15,000 = Free Original Collard Green Dip.
    🧒 Kid mode (kid-sized prizes from the regular menu): 3,000 = Free Fresh Squeezed OJ; 3,500 = Free side of French Friez; 4,250 = Free Sweet Gritz; 4,500 = Free Chocolate Chip Waffle. Kid scores are set so a typical child wins about as often as a typical adult does in Adult mode. Kid prize — redeemable with the young player at the table, one per kid per visit. Kid prize codes start with KID- and show a "🧒 KID PRIZE" tag. The larger prizes (Smackin' Mac, Fried Cheese Gritz, Collard Green Dip) are Adult mode only. One prize at a time per member; a prize must be used within 3 days.
  • Weekly top score: the highest score from Monday through Sunday close wins the weekly prize (sample: Free Wingz N' Wafflez + a karaoke shoutout). Ties go to the score posted first. The board resets every Monday.
  • Scores made with cheats, scripts, bots or multiple accounts are disqualified, and so are adult scores entered in Kid mode.
  • Kids' privacy: no data is collected from children. Kid mode only unlocks after a parent or guardian (18+) signs in on the phone with their own name, phone number and email and confirms they are the parent or guardian; the account and all contact information belong to the parent. The child never enters any information. Kid play, the kid best score and kid prizes are recorded on the parent's account, and kid prizes are claimed under it. An optional nickname for the score screen is stored only on that phone and is never sent anywhere. Leaving Kid mode, switching into it from Adult mode and signing out ask for the last 4 digits of the parent's phone (in the live app, a text code sent to the parent's phone and verified by the server).
