4. Route 66 is a game of skill

Route 66: Tulsa to Lomita is a driving and cooking game in six legs. Your score depends on how well you play: you steer the smoker rig between lanes to grab hickory logs, dry rub, sauce and meat and to dodge tumbleweeds, cones, potholes, deer and slow cars; pickups in a row without a hit build a combo (from the 5th in a row each pickup scores double, from the 10th triple, and a PERFECT pull while the combo is 10 or more adds 500 points; any hit resets it); at each stop you keep the smoker's needle in the right temperature band, pull the meat at the right moment and serve each order before its timer runs out. Each hit or walk-out costs one of 5 hearts. Pickups and hazards appear in a varied pattern, so every run is different, but no prize in the game is awarded by chance: prizes depend only on your score. Items shown in the game are for scoring only.

  • Adult score prizes (sample, set by the Sponsor), one run over: 5,000 = Free Cornbread w/ Honey Butter; 7,500 = Free side of Mac & Cheese or Mashed Potatoes; 9,500 = Free Aunt Cheryl's Peach Cobbler; 10,800 = Free Brisket Sandwich.
  • Kid mode (12 and under, unlocked only through a parent's or guardian's own account; the child never enters any information): 8,000 = Free Margot Dean's Homemade Cookie; 10,900 = Free Cornbread w/ Honey Butter. Kid prizes are saved to the parent's account and redeemed only with the young player at the table.
  • One prize at a time per member; a prize must be used within 3 days.
  • Weekly top score: the highest Adult-mode score from Monday through Sunday close wins a Free Brisket Sandwich + Aunt Cheryl's Peach Cobbler. Ties go to the score posted first. The board resets every Monday.
  • Scores made with cheats, scripts, bots or multiple accounts are disqualified.
