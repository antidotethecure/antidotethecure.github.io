Which one do I have?
  • Look at the card reader or screen. The brand name or logo is almost always printed on it: Toast (orange-red, "toast" in lowercase), Square (a white square-in-a-square), Clover (green four-leaf mark, "clover"), SpotOn, Lightspeed (a flame), Revel (usually an iPad on a stand).
  • Look at the bottom of a printed receipt. Most say "Powered by…" or show the company's name or web address.
  • Look at your bank deposits. Card sales are usually deposited under the name of the company that runs your POS.
  • Ask whoever opens the store. Whatever app they log into at the start of a shift is your POS.
  • Still not sure? Pick Something else. Those steps work on almost any system.
