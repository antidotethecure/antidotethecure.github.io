Run a truck? Get found.

No more "where's the truck?". Customers scan your QR sticker once. After that they can see you on a live map, their phone rings when you're close, and they know how long until you're on their street. Tap one to try it.

  • 📍Live map: the customer's house and exactly where the truck is

  • 🎁Check in 5 times → 10% off or an extra scoop; bonus points for keeping alerts on
