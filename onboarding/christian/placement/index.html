The rule

Food art + QR goes where people are deciding or waiting: the street window, the door, the hallway to the restrooms, the game room, the patio fence. QR only goes where people are already seated and ordering: tables, the bar, the check.

  2. Every new piece (door, bar, host, coaster, check, restroom, hall, patio) points at the short link https://antidotethecure.github.io/melody/?s=<source>, so the app can move without a reprint and each spot can be counted. Scan-verify each one with qrlib.verify.

What to avoid

  • Health code: no stickers on or near ice bins, bar wells, prep surfaces, the coffee/server station or anything behind the bar.
  • Safety and ADA: never cover exit signs, the ADA restroom signs, fire extinguishers, or the yellow ADA pads at the patio doors.
  • Never on screens: the self-order kiosk, the ATM, TVs or POS terminals.
  • Glare: the granite bar and glossy wood panels reflect. Order matte finishes and do not mount QRs right under the pendant lights.
  • Distance: a QR scans from about 10x its width. A 3 in code works within ~30 in; a 24x36 poster QR (~8 in) within ~6-7 ft. Nothing high up under the TVs.
  • Do not cover the menu or the OPEN/hours decal, and never put a QR on top of someone else's poster (Comedy Joint, Jack Daniel's).
  • One QR piece per table, max. Too many pieces reads as spam and lowers scans.
  • Sun and weather: patio and street-window pieces need UV laminate and outdoor vinyl or coroplast.
  • Pool tables: nothing on the felt or rails.
