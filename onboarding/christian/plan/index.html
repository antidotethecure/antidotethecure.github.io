⏱️ While we wait for Toast: interim mode

About 30 days · no staff phones

Guests can download the app, play the game, earn points and redeem rewards starting now. Your staff never pick up their own phones.

1
Redeeming: "show & tap." The guest taps Use reward on their phone. A live reward pass appears with a moving background, a ticking clock and a 10-minute countdown, so screenshots don't work. The points come off right away and it can only be used once. Your server looks at it and taps the matching preset discount button in Toast (for example "App Reward – Free Fries").
2
Earning: "snap the receipt." After paying, the guest taps Add my visit and takes a photo of the Toast receipt, or types the check # and total. They get 10 points per $1 right away. Limits: one claim per check, one per day, and a cap per claim.
3
You stay in control. A manager log shows every reward used and every receipt claim, with Approve and Flag buttons, so you or Sami can spot-check against Toast's daily sales report.
4
One-time setup, about 10 minutes: we add 4–5 reward discount buttons in Toast Web with you.

When Toast approves, earning becomes automatic and Redeem moves into Toast. Every guest's points and membership carry over. Interim mode costs nothing per month: your monthly billing still starts only after the Toast integration is approved, and the 90-day pilot starts then too.
