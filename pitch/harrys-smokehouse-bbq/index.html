The game (play it now)

ROUTE 66
TULSA ➜ LOMITA

Harry's story, played. Drive the smoker trailer down Route 66 from Tulsa to Lomita. On the road you grab brisket, ribs, hickory logs, dry rub and sauce. Then you pull up to a stop, take the customers' orders and cook the meat right on the smoker. Serve it fast and perfectly done for big points, then get back on the road to the next state.
