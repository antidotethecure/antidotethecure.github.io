For your tech team

  • No app store. It's a web app (PWA): guests scan a QR and it opens in the browser; they can add it to the Home Screen.
  • Works next to your POS. Phase 1: rewards ring up as discount buttons in your POS (Toast, Square, Clover) with one-time codes from the app (expire in minutes, one per visit, no stacking). Phase 2: a direct POS integration where the POS partner program allows it.
  • Customer data stays private. Member names, phones and emails never sit in public files. The owner back office needs a per-person login; staff tools never show contact info or numbers.
  • Texts are opt-in only, with STOP to unsubscribe and carrier registration.
  • Hosting, updates and support are included in the monthly price.
