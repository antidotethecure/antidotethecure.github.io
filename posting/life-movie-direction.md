# Life Movie — creative direction (locked by Antidote, 2026-09-17)

## The ending (non-negotiable)
The movie ENDS on this theme:

> After building up my revenue plan on GTA 5, I'm going out to Thailand
> to start playing GTA 6.

The arc closes forward-looking: GTA 5 grind → pockets got bigger → Thailand → GTA 6 era begins.

## Closing music cue
Use Antidote's song **"ATBS (Ain't Talkin' About Shit)"** — specifically the
section with the lyric:

> "I just played GTA 5 till my pockets got bigger.
>  Now I'm stunting, balling like I ought to be."

That lyric plays over the closing sequence. Time the reveal/last shots so this
bar lands on the final images (Thailand / GTA 6 tease), then end.

## THE TRAILER — "The Rewind" (locked by Antidote, 2026-09-17)

Concept: dark, dope art. The trailer OPENS at the ending and REWINDS the whole
life back to the beginning.

Structure (~60–75s):
1. **Open at the peak** (3–5s): the ending image — Thailand / GTA 6 era /
   "stunting, balling like I ought to be" playing clean.
2. **The rewind hits** (record-scratch / tape-rewind effect, audio reverses):
   everything starts pulling backward.
3. **Rewind through the chapters, newest to oldest** — each one gets a beat:
   - The GTA 5 grind / streams / revenue coming up
   - Homelessness
   - County jail
   - Prison
   - The fights
   - The heartbreaks
   - The gunshots
4. **Land at the beginning** (earliest childhood photo, hold in silence).
5. **Smash to black. Title card**, then one line: the movie is coming.

Art direction: dark graphic-novel / noir treatment — high contrast, grain,
black with one accent color (blood red or money green), ink/spray texture,
VHS glitch frames during the rewind. Real photos from the 43K library get the
dark-art treatment; AI art used only for chapter cards / transitions, never
to fabricate life events.

Audio: ATBS reversed/warped during the rewind, snapping back to the clean
"pockets got bigger" bar only at the very start (the peak) — so the trailer
teases the lyric the full movie ends on.
