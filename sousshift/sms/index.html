How to opt in

You can opt in either way:

  1. Text us first. Send any message (or the word START) to (424) 296-3303. Your first message is your consent to receive replies and account messages from SousShift AI Owner Support.
  2. Check the box on your owner onboarding page. Every client's private onboarding page has the opt-in shown below. It is never pre-checked.

We only text business clients who opted in. We never buy or rent phone lists, and we don't send marketing to people who haven't opted in.

What we send

  • Replies to your questions and concerns
  • Setup steps and launch updates for your app
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Billing and payment reminders

Message frequency varies, usually a few messages a week. Message and data rates may apply.

Sample messages:

  • "SousShift AI: Hi Christian, your Toast reward buttons are ready to add. Want to do it together tomorrow at 10am? Reply STOP to opt out."
  • "SousShift AI: Got your message about the menu photos. Antidote will reply within a few hours. Reply HELP for help."

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You'll get one confirmation and then no more texts. Reply START to join again. Reply HELP for help, or email secondshiftai@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
