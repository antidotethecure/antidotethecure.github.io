## APEX roadster model (assets/apex/apex_hi.glb, apex_lo.glb)
Generated by us (SousShift AI) with Higgsfield / Tripo H3.1 (single image) from the owner's own reference reel frame (an unbranded concept
car), then optimised with gltf-transform (dedup, weld, simplify to ~15k / ~5k triangles, meshopt, WebP 1024 / 512). Owned by the
project; no third-party license. No badges, logos or names.

## Street cars (assets/cars2/*.glb)
ev_sedan, muscle_coupe, compact_sedan, midsize_sedan, steel_pickup: generated by us (SousShift AI) via Higgsfield / Tripo from our own
studio renders (the pickup from a text prompt), then simplified to ~2.6–2.8k triangles, base-colour only at 512 px WebP, meshopt
(gltf-transform). Front/rear badges and wheel-centre caps were painted out of the textures; resemblance is by shape and colour only,
no brand names or logos. Owned by the project; no third-party license.

## Harry's Route 66 (demo/harrys-bbq/img/r66/)
Cook-stop food photos (`cook/*-raw.webp`, `cook/*-done.webp`), guests (`guest1-5.webp`, generic AI people, no real person's likeness),
smoke puffs (`smoke1-3.webp`), the deer (`deer.webp`) and the ground textures (`ground-grass.webp`, `ground-dry.webp`,
`ground-desert.webp`): generated by us (SousShift AI) with Higgsfield (nano_banana_2_1), the food matched to Harry's real dishes.
Owned by the project; no third-party license. The road, shoulders, shadows and rings are drawn in code (`route66-3d.js`).
The pickup, smoker trailer and three traffic cars (`demo/harrys-bbq/models/*.glb`) are meshes generated by us (SousShift AI) with Higgsfield
from our own prompts: reference images with gpt_image_2_6, meshes with meshy_v7_image_to_3d, then optimized with gltf-transform
(meshopt + WebP). Generic vehicles, no real make, badge or plate. Owned by the project; no third-party meshes. The cone and the
stand-in models used when a mesh fails to load are built in code (`route66-models.js`).
The pickup's four wheels were cut out of its mesh into separate parts with our own script (triangles inside each wheel's
cylinder moved to a `wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr` node), so they roll and the front pair steers. Same mesh, no new asset.
three.js r160 from cdn.jsdelivr.net (MIT).
